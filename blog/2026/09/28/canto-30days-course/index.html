30-Day Hong Kong Cantonese Course​

How to use this course​

Aim for 30–45 minutes per day:

10 min: vocabulary

10 min: say the example sentences aloud

10 min: review previous days

5–15 min: listening/speaking practice

I'll use Jyutping:

我 ngo5 = I / me

The number represents the Cantonese tone.

Week 1 — Survival Cantonese​

Day 1 — Hello! 你好​

Vocabulary

CantoneseJyutpingEnglish

Learn these chunks

你好！ nei5 hou2 Hello!

早晨！ zou2 san4 Good morning!

多謝！ do1 ze6 Thank you!

唔該！ m4 goi1 Thanks / excuse me. Mini-dialogue

A: 你好！ Hello!

B: 你好！早晨！ Hello! Good morning!

A: 多謝！ Thanks! Today's mission

Say 你好、早晨、多謝、唔該 aloud 10 times each.

Day 2 — I, you, he/she​

Vocabulary

CantoneseJyutpingEnglish
我ngo5I
你nei5you
佢keoi5he / she
我哋ngo5 dei6we
你哋nei5 dei6you plural
佢哋keoi5 dei6they
人jan4person
朋友pang4 jau5friend
老師lou5 si1teacher
學生hok6 saang1student

Key pattern

我係…… ngo5 hai6 ... I am...

你係…… nei5 hai6 ... You are...

Examples:

我係學生。 ngo5 hai6 hok6 saang1 I'm a student.

佢係我朋友。 keoi5 hai6 ngo5 pang4 jau5 He/she is my friend. Day 3 — The most important negative words

Today is extremely important.

Vocabulary

CantoneseJyutpingEnglish
係hai6be
唔係m4 hai6isn't / am not
有jau5have
冇mou5don't have
唔m4not
知zi1know
明ming4understand
識sik1know / be familiar with

Key distinction

唔 m4 generally negates actions, descriptions, etc.

我唔知。 ngo5 m4 zi1 I don't know.

冇 mou5 means don't have / there isn't.

我冇錢。 ngo5 mou5 cin2 I don't have money.

Essential survival sentence

我唔明。 ngo5 m4 ming4 I don't understand. Day 4 — What?

Vocabulary

CantoneseJyutpingEnglish

邊個bin1 go3who

嗰個go2 go3that

Pattern

呢個係咩？ ni1 go3 hai6 me1? What is this?

嗰個係咩？ go2 go3 hai6 me1? What's that?

邊個？ bin1 go3? Who? Day 5 — Where?

Vocabulary

CantoneseJyutpingEnglish

你住邊度？ nei5 zyu6 bin1 dou6? Where do you live?

廁所喺邊度？ ci3 so2 hai2 bin1 dou6? Where's the toilet? Day 6 — Numbers 1–10

Day 7 — Review + first conversation​

You now know enough to make a tiny conversation. Dialogue

A: 你好！你叫咩名呀？ Hello! What's your name?

B: 我叫 Alex。你呢？ My name is Alex. And you?

A: 我叫 Sam。 My name is Sam.

B: 你係邊度人？ Where are you from?

A: 我係英國人。 I'm British. Week 1 challenge

Without looking at the English:

Introduce yourself.

Say where you're from.

Say where you live.

Ask someone's name.

Say you don't understand.

Day 14 — Week 2 review​

Challenge

Have this conversation aloud:

Hello. What's your name? Where are you from? Where do you live? Are you hungry? What do you want to eat? How much is it? Thank you. See you tomorrow.

Don't worry about speed. Accuracy first.

Day 21 — Week 3 review​

Scenario

You are lost in Hong Kong.

Ask:

Where is the MTR station?

Can you help me?

Is it far?

Which way?

Thank the person.

Try to do the whole interaction without English.

Week 4 — Real Cantonese​

Now we move from vocabulary to natural speech.

Day 22 — Sentence particles​

These are essential.

ParticleJyutpingRough function
呀aa3conversational / question
啦laa1urging / change
喎wo3new information
嘅ge3emphasis / possession
㗎gaa3emphasis / assurance
囉lo1obvious conclusion
咩me1surprise / challenge
啫ze1only / just

Compare:

食飯。 Eat.

食飯呀？ Are you eating?

食飯啦。 Let's eat / Go eat.

食咗飯喇。 I've eaten already.

Don't try to perfectly translate particles. Learn their feel and usage.

Day 23 — Aspect: 咗​

咗 zo2 often indicates a completed action or changed state. Pattern

Verb + 咗

食咗 sik6 zo2 ate / have eaten

去咗 heoi3 zo2 went / have gone

買咗 maai5 zo2 bought Example

我食咗飯喇。 ngo5 sik6 zo2 faan6 laa3 I've eaten already.

Day 24 — Aspect: 緊​

緊 gan2 indicates an action in progress. Pattern

Verb + 緊

食緊 eating

睇緊 watching / looking at

做緊 doing

返緊工 working Example

我食緊飯。 ngo5 sik6 gan2 faan6 I'm eating. Day 25 — “Want to” Pattern

想 + verb

我想去香港。 ngo5 soeng2 heoi3 hoeng1 gong2 I want to go to Hong Kong.

我想飲咖啡。 I want to drink coffee.

你想食咩？ What do you want to eat? Challenge

Make five sentences beginning:

我想……

Day 26 — Because / therefore / but​

CantoneseJyutpingEnglish
因為jan1 wai6because
所以so2 ji5therefore / so
但係daan6 hai6but
如果jyu4 gwo2if
同tung4with / and
或者waak6 ze2or / maybe

Example

因為今日落雨，所以我唔出去。

jan1 wai6 gam1 jat6 lok6 jyu5, so2 ji5 ngo5 m4 ceot1 heoi3

Because it's raining today, I'm not going out.

These make your Cantonese sound much more conversational.

Example

Now combine everything. Dialogue

A: 你今日做咩呀？ What are you doing today?

B: 我今日返工。你呢？ I'm working today. And you?

A: 我唔使返工，想去飲咖啡。 I don't have to work, so I want to go for coffee.

B: 好呀！去邊度？ Sure! Where?

A: 去附近嗰間咖啡店。 To that coffee shop nearby.

B: 得喎！ Sounds good!

Notice how little of this is made from complicated vocabulary. Common words + particles + sentence patterns are the key.

Day 29 — Survival conversation test​

Try answering these without looking anything up. Introduction

你叫咩名？ What's your name?

你係邊度人？ Where are you from?

你住邊度？ Where do you live? Food

你食咩呀？ What are you eating?

你想飲咩？ What do you want to drink? Navigation

你去邊度？ Where are you going?

地鐵站喺邊度？ Where's the MTR station? Shopping

呢個幾多錢？ How much is this?

有冇平啲？ Is there a cheaper one? Understanding

你明唔明？ huge difference once you start listening to actual Hong Kong Cantonese. Do you understand?

我唔明。 I don't understand. Day 30 — Your first Cantonese conversation 🎉

By now, try to perform this entire dialogue aloud. Meeting someone

A: 你好！ Hello!

B: 你好！你叫咩名呀？ Hello! What's your name?

A: 我叫 Alex。你呢？ I'm Alex. And you?

B: 我叫 Sam。你係邊度人？ I'm Sam. Where are you from?

A: 我係英國人，而家住喺東京。你呢？ I'm British, and I live in Tokyo now. You?

B: 我係香港人。 I'm from Hong Kong.

A: 你今日做咩呀？ What are you doing today?

B: 我今日唔使返工，想去食飯。 I don't have to work today, so I want to go eat.

A: 好呀！你想食咩？ Sure! What do you want to eat?

B: 我想食點心。你呢？ I want to eat dim sum. You?

A: 我都想食！ I want to eat it too!

B: 咁我哋一齊去啦！ Then let's go together!

A: 好呀！ Sure!

🎯 Your 30-day targets​

By the end of the course, aim to be able to:

Introduce yourself.

Ask someone's name and where they're from.

Say where you live.

Order food and drinks.

Ask for the bill.

Ask prices.

Buy something.

Ask for directions.

Use the MTR/bus/taxi vocabulary.

Ask someone to repeat or help you.

Say you don't understand.

Talk about what you're doing.

Talk about yesterday/today/tomorrow.

Express wants and needs.

Use 唔 and 冇 correctly.

Begin using 咗 and 緊.

Recognize common sentence particles.

Hold a basic 2–5 minute conversation.

The 20 patterns I'd memorize first

If you remember nothing else from the course, remember these:

我係…… — I am...

我唔係…… — I am not...

我有…… — I have...

我冇…… — I don't have...

我想…… — I want to...

我要…… — I want/need...

我唔要…… — I don't want...

我唔知。 — I don't know.

我唔明。 — I don't understand.

你識唔識……？ — Do you know...?

你食咗飯未呀？ — Have you eaten yet?

你做緊咩呀？ — What are you doing?

你去邊度？ — Where are you going?

呢個係咩？ — What is this?

幾多錢呀？ — How much is it?

可唔可以……？ — Can I...?

得唔得呀？ — Is that okay?

喺邊度？ — Where is it?

唔該…… — Please / excuse me...

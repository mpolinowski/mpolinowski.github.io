30-Day Hong Kong Cantonese Course​

How to use this course​

Aim for 30–45 minutes per day:

10 min: vocabulary

10 min: say the example sentences aloud

10 min: review previous days

5–15 min: listening/speaking practice

I'll use Jyutping:

我 ngo5 = I / me

The number represents the Cantonese tone.

Today is extremely important.

Vocabulary

CantoneseJyutpingEnglish
係hai6be
唔係m4 hai6isn't / am not
有jau5have
冇mou5don't have
唔m4not
知zi1know
明ming4understand
識sik1know / be familiar with

Key distinction

唔 m4 generally negates actions, descriptions, etc.

我唔知。 ngo5 m4 zi1 I don't know.

冇 mou5 means don't have / there isn't.

我冇錢。 ngo5 mou5 cin2 I don't have money.

Essential survival sentence

我唔明。 ngo5 m4 ming4 I don't understand. Day 4 — What?

Week 4 — Real Cantonese​

Now we move from vocabulary to natural speech.

Day 22 — Sentence particles​

These are essential.

ParticleJyutpingRough function
呀aa3conversational / question
啦laa1urging / change
喎wo3new information
嘅ge3emphasis / possession
㗎gaa3emphasis / assurance
囉lo1obvious conclusion
咩me1surprise / challenge
啫ze1only / just

Compare:

食飯。 Eat.

食飯呀？ Are you eating?

食飯啦。 Let's eat / Go eat.

食咗飯喇。 I've eaten already.

Don't try to perfectly translate particles. Learn their feel and usage.

Day 23 — Aspect: 咗​

咗 zo2 often indicates a completed action or changed state. Pattern

Verb + 咗

食咗 sik6 zo2 ate / have eaten

去咗 heoi3 zo2 went / have gone

買咗 maai5 zo2 bought Example

我食咗飯喇。 ngo5 sik6 zo2 faan6 laa3 I've eaten already.

Day 24 — Aspect: 緊​

緊 gan2 indicates an action in progress. Pattern

Verb + 緊

食緊 eating

睇緊 watching / looking at

做緊 doing

返緊工 working Example

我食緊飯。 ngo5 sik6 gan2 faan6 I'm eating. Day 25 — “Want to” Pattern

想 + verb

我想去香港。 ngo5 soeng2 heoi3 hoeng1 gong2 I want to go to Hong Kong.

我想飲咖啡。 I want to drink coffee.

你想食咩？ What do you want to eat? Challenge

Make five sentences beginning:

我想……

Now combine everything. Dialogue

A: 你今日做咩呀？ What are you doing today?

B: 我今日返工。你呢？ I'm working today. And you?

A: 我唔使返工，想去飲咖啡。 I don't have to work, so I want to go for coffee.

B: 好呀！去邊度？ Sure! Where?

A: 去附近嗰間咖啡店。 To that coffee shop nearby.

B: 得喎！ Sounds good!

Notice how little of this is made from complicated vocabulary. Common words + particles + sentence patterns are the key.

By now, try to perform this entire dialogue aloud. Meeting someone

A: 你好！ Hello!

B: 你好！你叫咩名呀？ Hello! What's your name?

A: 我叫 Alex。你呢？ I'm Alex. And you?

B: 我叫 Sam。你係邊度人？ I'm Sam. Where are you from?

A: 我係英國人，而家住喺東京。你呢？ I'm British, and I live in Tokyo now. You?

B: 我係香港人。 I'm from Hong Kong.

A: 你今日做咩呀？ What are you doing today?

B: 我今日唔使返工，想去食飯。 I don't have to work today, so I want to go eat.

A: 好呀！你想食咩？ Sure! What do you want to eat?

B: 我想食點心。你呢？ I want to eat dim sum. You?

A: 我都想食！ I want to eat it too!

B: 咁我哋一齊去啦！ Then let's go together!

A: 好呀！ Sure!

🎯 Your 30-day targets​

By the end of the course, aim to be able to:

Introduce yourself.

Ask someone's name and where they're from.

Say where you live.

Order food and drinks.

Ask for the bill.

Ask prices.

Buy something.

Ask for directions.

Use the MTR/bus/taxi vocabulary.

Ask someone to repeat or help you.

Say you don't understand.

Talk about what you're doing.

Talk about yesterday/today/tomorrow.

Express wants and needs.

Use 唔 and 冇 correctly.

Begin using 咗 and 緊.

Recognize common sentence particles.

Hold a basic 2–5 minute conversation.

What is meant by CI/CD?​

This is a method of regularly delivering apps to customers and automating all phases of application development. The main concepts of CI/CD are Continuous Integration, Continuous Delivery and Continuous Deployment. CI/CD solves the problems that integrating new code can cause for DevOps teams (also known as "integration hell").

In particular, CI/CD ensures continuous automation and monitoring throughout the entire app lifecycle, from the integration and testing to the deployment and implementation phase. These interrelated practices are often referred to as the "CI/CD pipeline" and are supported by agile collaboration between DevOps teams.

Search & Analytics Backend (Elasticsearch)

Project information

  • Category: DevOps
  • Client: INSTAR Deutschland GmbH (Waletech) Shenzhen, China
  • Project date: 01 March, 2018
  • Project URL: www.elastic.co

Elasticsearch is the search and analytics backbone for three separate products: the customer knowledge base (full-text search across documentation and FAQ), the AI prediction layer (storing and querying detection events, plate reads, and face-match results), and the internal SysAdmin dashboard. I own the index schema, the ingestion pipelines that feed it from the AI chain and the ticketing system, and the performance tuning that keeps p95 search latency under 200 ms at fleet scale.
